A luxury real estate brand aiming to connect high-end buyers to their dream home, be it for ownership or investment.

Timeline

7 weeks (2025)

Deliverables

Brand Identity, Web Design & Marketing Collateral

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Context

House of Tris is a luxury real estate brand focused on presenting exceptional properties through a refined, trustworthy and contemporary identity. The brand needed a visual direction that would appeal to high-end buyers while communicating professionalism, exclusivity and confidence.

Goals

Create a sophisticated and cohesive brand system that strengthens House of Tris's market positioning, elevates property presentation and establishes a consistent visual language across digital and marketing touchpoints.

DESIGN APPROACH

Research & Discovery

The design process focused on understanding the luxury real estate market and identifying visual opportunities for House of Tris to establish a distinctive yet timeless presence. Research explored premium property brands, typography, colour imagery and contemporary luxury aesthetics.

Key Design Decisions

  • PREMIUM VISUAL IDENTITY

    A sophisticated visual language was developed using elegant typography, considered spacing and a restrained colour palette to create a sense of quality and exclusivity.

  • CLEAR COMMUNICATION

    The identity and supporting materials were designed to keep property information clear and accessible while allowing imagery and key details to remain the focus.

  • CONSISTENT BRAND SYSTEM

    A flexible visual system was established to ensure House of Tris could maintain a recognisable and consistent presence across property marketing, social media and digital platforms.
