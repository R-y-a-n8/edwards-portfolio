PAM GOLDING PROPERTIES

Pam Golding Properties Kenya is a leading real estate company specialising in residential and commercial sales, rentals, and property management across Nairobi and the Coast. Backed by a team of over 100 professionals and a strong portfolio of managed properties, the company is recognised for its market expertise, service excellence, and trusted relationships with homeowners, investors, developers, and corporate clients.

TITLE

Role/ Purpose:

To develop and deliver high-quality visual communication and marketing materials that strengthen the Pam Golding Properties Kenya brand, support sales initiatives, and enhance the presentation of properties, agents, and company services.

Deliverable:

  • Design and production of all marketing collateral, including brochures, flyers, magazines, presentations, advertisements, and digital assets.
  • Visual branding and consistency across all Pam Golding Properties Kenya communications.
  • Marketing support for agents, management, and corporate initiatives.
  • Coordination of artwork approvals and management of design workflows through project management systems.
  • Development of creative concepts and campaigns to enhance property marketing and brand visibility.
  • Matterport scanning, recording, editing, and publishing of property virtual tours to enhance property marketing.
  • In-house photography for properties, corporate events, and other marketing requirements as needed.
  • Management of print-ready artwork and liaison with suppliers where required.
  • Design and development of email marketing templates and campaigns to support property and corporate communications.

PRINT & DIGITAL PUBLICATIONS

EXQUISITE

From Exquisite issue 10 through to issue 17, I have led the creative production of Pam Golding Properties Kenya's bi-annual luxury magazine. One of my favourite projects, it showcases my ability to produce a polished editorial publication that reflects the brand's premium identity through thoughtful design and compelling property presentation. Below are online links to the last 3 issues.

COASTAL COLLECTION

Following the opening of our coastal branch in 2023, the Coastal Collection started as a digital publication before expanding into print. It helps showcase properties while building agent visibility and positioning them as area specialists. The digital collection is now published quarterly.

JUST LISTED

My manager and I developed the Just Listed digital collection to generate interest in newly listed rental properties. Published weekly, the series has gained increasing traction across our platforms since Issue 1 and has become a valuable marketing tool for rental agents.

MATTERPORT SCANNING AND EDITING & PHOTOGRAPHY

MATTERPORT VIRTUAL TOURS

I capture, process, and edit Matterport property scans to create seamless, immersive virtual walkthroughs. The tours allow clients, including those unable to attend physical viewings, to explore properties remotely, while providing agents with valuable property measurements and details.
