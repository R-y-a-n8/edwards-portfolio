Quick Harvest

A comprehensive brand identity and digital product design for a sustainable agriculture platform, connecting local farmers with urban consumers.

Role

Brand Designer, UI/UX Designer

Timeline

7 weeks (2025)

Deliverables

Brand Identity, Web Design & Marketing

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Context

Quick Harvest is a startup building a platform to connect local farmers directly with urban consumers who want fresh, sustainable produce. The company needed a brand identity that felt approachable and trustworthy while communicating their commitment to sustainability and quality.

Goals

Create a cohesive visual system that appeals to both farmers and consumers, design an intuitive digital experience for ordering and delivery tracking, and establish brand guidelines for future marketing efforts.

DESIGN APPROACH

Research & Discovery

I began by interviewing both farmers and target consumers to understand their needs, pain points, and perceptions around local food systems. This research revealed that trust, transparency, and simplicity were crucial for both audiences.

Key Design Decisions

  • Natural & Approachable Visual Language

    The brand uses earthy greens and warm neutrals to evoke freshness and sustainability without feeling corporate or clinical. Typography is friendly yet confident, balancing professionalism with accessibility.

  • Clear Information Hierarchy

    The digital interface prioritizes product imagery and farmer profiles, making it easy for consumers to see where their food comes from. Navigation is straightforward, with a streamlined checkout process.

  • Scalable Design System

    All brand elements were designed to work across digital and print touchpoints—from packaging and market signage to social media and the web platform.
